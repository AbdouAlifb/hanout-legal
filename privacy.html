Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

OPEN LLUNA ("we") operates the hanout mobile app, a grocery, pharmacy, beauty and bakery delivery service in Morocco. This policy explains what we collect and why. We process personal data in line with Moroccan Law 09-08 (CNDP).

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, phone number (optional), password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Delivery: delivery addresses and instructions.
  • Business accounts: trade name, ICE, trade-register number and document, accounts email.
  • Orders: products, quantities, prices, delivery slot, payment method, order status and ratings.
  • Device: a push-notification token, and crash reports (no personal data).

Why

To create your account, take and deliver your orders, issue invoices to businesses, send order updates and offers (you can turn notifications off in Profile), provide support, and keep the service secure.

Sharing

With the people who deliver your order (name, masked phone, address), our hosting and authentication provider (Supabase, EU data centres), our email provider, our push provider (Expo / Apple / Google), and authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete your account at any time in Profile › Delete account. We then remove your name, contact details, addresses, favourites and bag, and disable your login. Order and invoice records are kept in anonymised form for as long as accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or object to processing, by writing to contact@cardnd.com. You may also contact the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

Children

hanout is not directed at children under 16.
