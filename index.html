hanout support

Groceries, pharmacy, beauty and bakery, delivered in Casablanca, for homes and shops.

Contact us

Email contact@cardnd.com. We reply within one business day. Please include your order number (for example #HN-24802) if your question is about an order.

Orders and delivery

Track every order live in the app under Profile › Orders. If something is missing or damaged, write to us within 48 hours of delivery.

Business (wholesale) accounts

Shops, cafés and pharmacies can apply for a business account in the app with their ICE and trade-register document. Once verified, wholesale prices, case quantities, invoices and payment terms are unlocked.

Delete your hanout account

You can delete your hanout account and its data at any time:

  1. Open the hanout app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile › Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app? Email contact@cardnd.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my hanout account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Deleted: your name, email, phone number, delivery addresses, saved items, bag, notification settings and push token, and for business accounts the trade-register document. Your login is disabled.

Kept: order and invoice records, in anonymised form (no name or contact details), for as long as Moroccan accounting law requires (up to 10 years).
